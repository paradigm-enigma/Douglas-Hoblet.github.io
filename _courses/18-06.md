If you do any flavor of CS, stats, or engineering at MIT you will use the
contents of this class forever. The version taught today is descended from
Prof. Strang's famous lecture series on OCW.

## What it's about

- Systems of linear equations, matrices, \\(LU\\) and \\(QR\\) factorizations.
- Vector spaces, four fundamental subspaces.
- Eigendecomposition and SVD.
- Symmetric, positive definite matrices.

## My take

If you watch Strang's OCW lectures *before* each live lecture, office hours
become a luxury. Do the pencil-and-paper problems. "I understood the video"
is a trap.

## Sister class decision

If you want the more proof-heavy version, take **18.700** instead.
18.06 is the computational one; 18.700 is for would-be mathematicians.
