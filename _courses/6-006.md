The canonical MIT intro algorithms class. Covers data structures, graph algorithms,
dynamic programming, and the analysis toolkit you'll use in every later CS class.

## What it's about

- Asymptotic analysis, amortization.
- Sorting, hashing, search trees.
- Graph algorithms: BFS/DFS, shortest paths, MST.
- Dynamic programming from first principles.
- A taste of NP-hardness.

## My take

6.006 is the class that teaches you how to *think* about running time,
not just memorize algorithms. The psets are the point — do them honestly,
and office hours exist for a reason.
