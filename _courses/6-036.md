The undergrad ML onramp at MIT — supervised learning
from the ground up, with math.

## What it's about

- Linear classifiers, the perceptron, SGD.
- Regularization, overfitting, cross-validation.
- Feature representations, kernels.
- Neural networks and backpropagation.
- Recurrent and convolutional architectures.
- A taste of reinforcement learning.

## My take

6.036 is *math-first*, which was unexpected and great. Understanding the loss
surface and the update rule before touching PyTorch gave me instincts I'd
never have picked up from tutorials.
