The new-number replacement for 6.009. Bigger projects, fewer hand-holding moments.
Teaches you how to think about code at the level of *programs*, not functions.

## What it's about

- Six labs that each build a nontrivial program (image editor, circuit simulator,
  Bacon-number graph explorer, Lisp interpreter, SAT solver, snekoban solver).
- Python throughout.
- Style and debugging as first-class topics.

## My take

The labs are 60% of the learning. Don't start the day before. The Lisp interpreter
lab was the moment I first felt like I was *writing software* rather than solving
homework problems.
