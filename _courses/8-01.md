GIR (General Institute Requirement). Newtonian mechanics from first principles.
Everyone at MIT takes some version of this.

## Variants offered

- **8.01** — standard lecture + recitation.
- **8.011 / 8.012** — slower and faster versions; 8.012 is for people who
  want a calculus-heavy, proof-style ride.
- **8.01L** — covers the same material over a longer calendar.

## What it's about

- Kinematics and Newton's laws.
- Energy, momentum, collisions.
- Rotational motion, torque, angular momentum.
- Simple harmonic motion, gravitation.

Draw the free-body diagram. Always. "I can do it in my head" is the #1
